Henan Ihwan Aluminum Industry Technology Co., Ltd. is an aluminum products company that integrates design and
development, automated manufacturing, and marketing. Originally established in year 2019, the company settled in the
industrial cluster area of Huaiyang District, Zhoukou City, Henan Province, through investment attraction in 2023. It
covers an area of 10,000 square meters, with a factory area of 8,000 square meters. The company has a registered capital
of 2 million USD and paid-in capital of 2 million USD. It has obtained the qualification certificate for foreign trade
operators, and has completed the registration for import and export enterprise code and customs registration.
Henan Ihwan Aluminum Industry Technology Co., Ltd holds an entire of fully automated production lines, including
aluminum foil slitting lines, aluminum foil cutting lines, aluminum foil container lines, aluminum foil wrapping paper lines,
silicone paper lines, hookah roll lines, and hookah tablet production lines, among others. Additionally, it possesses
advanced supporting equipment for plastic sealing and packaging. The main products include aluminum foil rolls,
aluminum foil containers, aluminum foil wrapping paper, hookah foil, battery foil, and other related aluminum foil products.
Henan Ihwan Aluminum Industry Technology Co., Ltd. provides processing services for aluminum foil products to
numerous top brands both domestically and internationally, and it is also committed to the research and development of
various aluminum foil deep processing equipment. Its products are well-received in both domestic and international
markets, exported to regions such as Europe, South America, North America, the Middle East, East Asia, and Southeast
Asia. The company has earned consistent praise from clients for its high-quality products, excellent service, and
reasonable prices, establishing strong cooperative relationships with many customers.
All of Henan Ihwan Aluminum Industry Technology Co., Ltd’s products is made from internationally recognized eco-
friendly high-quality aluminum foil materials that comply with established international hygiene standards. The products
have passed ISO 9001 management system certification, SGS quality testing, and third-party food safety testing.During
the process of production and using, It will not cause environmental pollution, and defective products can be recycled,
achieving sustainable development.

河南伊赫万铝业科技有限公司是一家集设计研发、自动化生产制造和市场营销于一
体的铝制品企业。原公司于2019年成立 后以招商引资的方式于2023年落户于河南省周
口市准阳区产业集聚区，占地面积10000平方米，厂房面积8000平方米，公司注册资本
200万美元，实收资本200万美元，已取得对外贸易经营者资格证书，并办理了进出口企
业代码和海关注册登记证。
河南伊赫万铝业科技有限公司拥有完整的全自动铝箔分条生产线、铝箔切片生产
线、铝箔餐盒生产线、铝箔锡纸、硅油纸、水烟卷、水烟片生产线等几十余条全自动生
产线，以及先进的塑封、包装等附属配套设备。主要生产铝箔卷材，铝箔容器，铝箔锡
纸，水烟箔，电池箔等相关铝箔制品。
河南伊赫万铝业科技有限公司长期为国内外诸多一流品牌提供铝箔制品加工服务，
同时致力于研制开发各类铝箔深加工设备，且产品畅销海内外，远销至欧洲、南美、北
美、中东、东亚、东南亚等地区，并以高质量的产品、优质的服务和合理的价格获得了
国内外客户的一致好评，与许多客户建立了良好的合作关系。
河南伊赫万铝业科技有限公司所有产品均采用国际通用环保优质铝箔材料，符合国
际制定卫生标准，并且已通过ISO9001管理体系及SGS质量检测及第三方食品检测认
证。在生产和使用过程中，不会对环境造成污染，且残次品可以回收再利用，实现了循
环可持续性发展。
